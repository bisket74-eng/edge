How This Build Plays
1
Event
Reveal the next shuffled Plot/Location Event and resolve it for every colony.
2
Action
In first-player order, spend every Action you have. Play Actions, build with Supplies, discard cards, use Survivor abilities, or end your turn. Refill after your turn.
3
Trade
Secretly commit cards showing TRADE. Highest valid bids choose two-card Trade piles first. Every card used in a valid bid is trashed.
4
Recruit
Recruit one Survivor from your hand by trashing Supply cards worth at least its red COST, or pass.
5
Cleanup
The marked Unlucky Survivor is lost unless rescued, the row and Trade piles are maintained, Weapons may reload, and the first-player marker moves.

Scenarios and decks

Choose Epidemic/New York, Nuclear Aftermath/New Mexico, or Alien Invasion/Nebraska in the lobby. Each game randomly removes one of the six matching Plot cards and one of the six matching Location cards, shuffles the remaining ten, removes one more unseen card, and plays with the remaining nine Events. Personal, Main, and Survivor decks are shuffled separately.

Every player receives the same ten-card personal deck: two Supply Crates, two Supply Crates, Safe House, Looting, Fire Camp, First Aid Kit, Scouting, and Adrenaline Shot.

Winning

The game ends during Cleanup when any colony has at least 11 Survival Points or the final Event has been played. Highest SP wins; remaining Life breaks a tie.

About the cards

The names, printed values, deck quantities, and abilities in this build are based on the rulebook and card photographs supplied for the original game and its three scenarios. The digital artwork and layout remain original to this app.

Card Badge Key

The badges are written out so no symbol has to be guessed:

COST 3Red: supplies required to recruit a Survivor or complete a Building.SUPPLY 2Green: supplies this card provides toward construction or recruitment.SP 4Light badge: Survival Points earned when the Survivor is recruited or Building is completed.TRADE 1Tan: value when the card is committed to a Trade bid.

Every Survivor: red COST + SP, plus TRADE only when that card has a printed trade value. Every Building: red COST + SP, plus TRADE only when printed. Every Supply: green SUPPLY, plus TRADE only when printed. Actions: TRADE only when printed. Event cards do not use these construction/recruitment badges.

Trade bids: You may select any card that displays a TRADE value. Supply, Supplies, and A Lot of Supplies currently have TRADE values of 1, 2, and 3 in this build, so they can be included in a bid.
