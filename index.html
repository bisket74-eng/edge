How This Build Plays
1
Event
A new event affects every colony. Read it, then the host begins the round.
2
Action
On your turn, play an Action, begin or supply a Building, discard cards and redraw, or rest.
3
Trade
Secretly commit cards with Trade value. Highest bids choose two-card trade piles first. Bid cards are trashed.
4
Recruit
Play one Survivor from your hand into your colony for its SP and ongoing ability, or pass.
5
Cleanup
The first-player marker moves, exhausted colonies recover, and the next event begins.

Winning

The prototype ends after eight rounds or when a colony reaches 20 Survival Points. Highest SP wins.

About the cards

This first index contains an original starter library so the multiplayer engine is playable. The card database is centralized in the code, making it straightforward to replace each placeholder with card data supplied from your own copy.
